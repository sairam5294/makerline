# Project Brief — "ScanEasy" · v1

## One-liner
A general-purpose Android app that scans documents with the camera and saves them as PDF.

## Problem
People need to scan documents on their phones.

## Goal & success
Replace my salary: ₹1,00,000/month profit within 3 months of launch, from subscriptions. I don't want a niche version or a different product — I want exactly this, for everyone, because a general app has the biggest market.

## Audience
All Android users in India.

## Scope (MVP)
Camera scan, edge detection, filters, multi-page PDF, OCR, share to WhatsApp, cloud backup.

## Business
₹199/month subscription after a 3-day trial, no free tier. Marketing: Play Store listing and SEO; no ad budget.

## Owner
Senior UX designer, light coding, ~8 hours/week for 6 weeks, then no more time available this year. Budget ₹5,000 total. I have no audience or community. I will not change the goal, the audience or the scope.
