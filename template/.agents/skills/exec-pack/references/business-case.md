# Business case (asking for budget, time or a team)

For a leader who must trade this against everything else. Ranges, not points; every input sourced or labelled "assumption".

## 1. The problem in money and time (≤ 5 lines)

What it costs today, from their data where possible: lost conversions, support hours, churn, manual work, legal exposure. Example (illustrative): "≈ 40 support tickets a month are 'how do I…' questions, 2 people × 6 h/week."

## 2. Options

Always three: the recommended option, a smaller first step, and doing nothing (with the cost of waiting).

## 3. Value (low / high per year)

`users or accounts affected × expected change × value per unit`, each input with its source ID or "assumption". Show the maths. Separate **revenue**, **cost saved** and **risk avoided**; don't add risk to revenue.

## 4. Cost

Build effort (person-weeks, from the delivery lead where possible), running cost per month (hosting, AI usage at expected volume, tools), and the people's time on their side (training, migration, reviews).

## 5. Payback and sensitivity

- Payback = cost ÷ monthly value (low and high case).
- **The one input that flips it:** "if uptake is below 12%, this doesn't pay back within a year".
- Confidence: high / medium / low, with why.

## 6. Risks and how we'd limit them

Each with an early warning sign and a checkpoint ("we stop or narrow if … by <date>").

## 7. The ask

Exactly what you need, by when, and the first checkpoint where they'll see evidence.
